Privacy policy

Private by design.

Effective September 4, 2026.

Shortcut Coach Lite

Shortcut Coach Lite provides a local shortcut library and presentation previews. It does not request Accessibility access, inspect controls in other apps, require an account, include advertising, or collect analytics.

Full Shortcut Coach

The full app processes supported pointer and Accessibility information locally to identify manual actions and suggest equivalent keyboard shortcuts. It does not transmit Accessibility snapshots, typed text, URLs, tab titles, filenames, file paths, or coaching history.

Storage and sharing

Preferences and coaching history remain on the Mac. Shortcut Coach does not sell or share personal data and has no cloud-sync or tracking service.
